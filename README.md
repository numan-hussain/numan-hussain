## The short version

- **2013**: started on Elance, building pixel-perfect WordPress themes from PSDs for clients I never met. Over eight years that became 250+ contracts on Upwork, and the work moved from pages to platforms.
- **2020**: joined Worldview International Group and led **Find Visa Counsellor** and **Worldview Pro** from MVP to live products on web, iOS and Android. At Staff Asia I ran a team of WordPress and Laravel developers.
- **2022**: founded **[Devute](https://devute.com)**, a UK software studio where a cross-functional team has delivered 700+ projects. I stepped back from the directorship in 2026 and now advise.
- **Today**: I consult, and I teach through **[Everskill](https://everskills.co.uk)** in Bangla and English.

## How I work

**Audit → Plan → Deliver → Hand over.** The same shape whether I am leading a team for a year or rebuilding one site in six weeks. I read the code, the analytics and the backlog first, write a short plan, deliver with weekly demos, and leave a team that can run it without me.
